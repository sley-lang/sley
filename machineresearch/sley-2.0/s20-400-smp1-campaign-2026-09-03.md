## Design brief

Contract: `docs/spec/SMP1.md` revision 1, ADR-0032, stage checker
`scripts/check_smp1_contract.py`.

- Frames are length-prefixed SCB1 standalone envelopes (tags 400 request,
  401 response, 402 hello) under a new `sley2.protocol-frame.v1` domain to
  be registered by S20-410; the negotiated `max_frame_bytes` is checked
  before allocation.
- Derived negotiation: greatest common version, first common epoch, minimum
  limits, intersected methods and features; the selected profile is
  digested under `sley2.protocol-handshake.v1`; `PROTOCOL_DOWNGRADE` is
  exact.
- Session-scoped strictly increasing request identifiers;
  `PROTOCOL_REQUEST_ID_CONFLICT` before execution; `SESSION_*` semantics
  stay with S20-330.
- Forty frozen method tags in six families, each naming the frozen
  contract that owns its bodies; reserved methods fail with a versioned
  reason.
- Bounded context copied from the owning response on every reply; no
  partial bodies; a failure envelope that preserves owner codes.
- Codes 40000 through 40011.
