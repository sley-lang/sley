## Independently re-derived claims

### Adapter insertion point (from the code, not the closeout)

A future optional ZJX transport adapter inserts at exactly three public
functions of `sley_repo` (crate root re-exports `exchange::*` at `lib.rs:21`):

1. `sley_repo::import_conformance_pack(store: &ObjectStore, input: &[u8], verifier: &V) -> Result<ImportReport>` (`crates/sley-repo/src/lib.rs:413`). Its body (lines 418 to 426) is `preflight_conformance_pack(input, verifier)?` (crate-private, `lib.rs:480`, S20-170 steps 1 to 5, no store access) followed by `promote_pack_objects(store, …)` (crate-private, `lib.rs:527`, step 6). The only path is the destination `ObjectStore` root; the artifact enters as bytes. `decode_envelope` (`lib.rs:689`) rejects `input.len() > MAX_PACK_BYTES` before reading a byte, bounds the payload length claim by `read_len(MAX_PACK_BYTES)`, and verifies the digest trailer against `RepositoryPackId::derive`.
2. `sley_repo::preflight_repository_exchange(input: &[u8], verifier: &V) -> Result<ExchangePreflightReport>` (`exchange.rs:1439`): pure `preflight(input, verifier)` with no path argument at all.
3. `sley_repo::import_repository_exchange(target: &Path, input: &[u8], verifier: &V) -> Result<ExchangeImportReport>` (`exchange.rs:1885`): `preflight(input, verifier)?` first (line 1890), then target classification, stage marker, exclusive maintenance, object promotion, receipts, branches, head. `decode_envelope` (`exchange.rs:645`) rejects `input.len() > MAX_EXCHANGE_BYTES` at entry.

Byte sources for the adapter's egress side are `export_conformance_pack` (`lib.rs:343`, returns `AcceptedRepositoryPack::stored_bytes`) and `export_repository_exchange` (`exchange.rs:954`, returns `AcceptedRepositoryExchange::stored_bytes`, embedding the pack via `export_conformance_pack` at line 1003 and bounding it at line 1004).

Why it is not a kernel dependency: `crates/sley-repo/Cargo.toml` depends on `sley-id, sley-mutate, sley-scb1, sley-schema, sley-state-root, sley-policy, sley-query, sley-ssmc, sley-store, sley-txn, blake3` only; the witness lives in `tests/` (a test target, compiled into no library or binary) and adds no dev-dependency. `dependency-closure.json` (27 normal / 30 all-kind external crates, none matching the compression, network, or dynamic-loading pattern) is consistent with `cargo metadata` at HEAD (lock file unchanged). A composition layer would depend on `sley-repo`; nothing in `sley-repo` or below can depend on it because no such crate exists and no feature or manifest changed.

Why it cannot bypass validation (stated from the code, and corrected against the closeout's wording, see finding 5): the accepted-fact types (`ImportReport` `lib.rs:224`, `AcceptedRepositoryPack` `lib.rs:207`, `AcceptedRepositoryExchange` `exchange.rs:369`, `ExchangeImportReport` `exchange.rs:407`) have all-`pub` fields and are therefore externally constructible, and `seal_mutated_conformance_pack_for_testing` (`lib.rs:461`, `pub`) builds an `AcceptedRepositoryPack` from caller-supplied entries; but constructing one confers nothing, because **no non-test API in the workspace consumes any of those values**: the only functions taking `&AcceptedRepositoryPack` or `&AcceptedRepositoryExchange` are inside `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` (`lib.rs:1318` after the `mod tests` at 1251; `exchange.rs:2308, 2739, 2762, 3003` after the `mod tests` at 1987), and the only workspace caller outside `sley-repo`, `crates/sley-protocol/src/server.rs:1838`, invokes `import_repository_exchange(&self.repository, body, &verifier)` with bytes. The sole routes by which bytes reach a store or a target are the three importers: `PreflightedPack` (`lib.rs:471`) and `promote_pack_objects` (`lib.rs:527`) are `pub(crate)`, so an external caller cannot reach step 6 without steps 1 to 5; the exchange persistence path (`exchange.rs:1890` onward) runs `preflight` first and then holds the exclusive maintenance guard and the incomplete-clone marker while `initialize_trusted_clone_*_with_maintenance` re-verifies receipts. Reconstructed bytes are therefore validated by the same `decode_envelope -> decode_payload -> validate_profile -> validate_epochs -> digest tree -> closure -> object verifier` chain as native bytes, and the witness's `adapter_import_*` helpers (witness lines 837 to 855) show the only admissible shape: reconstruct completely, then call the importer, with outer and inner failures kept as distinct variants.
