### Ownership and layering across records

The surface is coherent in code. The ownership record is not. `ENTITY_READ_PROFILE_V2.md:4,239` declares "Owner: S20-310 query semantics"; the oracle contract string is `s20-310-entity-read-oracle-v2`; the module docs call the owner a "Pure S20-310 owner"; REQ-04 files the review under the S20-310 register section. On the other side, every S20-310 record surface omits entity reads: `ROOT_BACKED_QUERY_PROFILE_V1.md` (composes restricted, S20-300, S20-250, STATE_ROOT, and nothing else), ADR-0030, the S20-310 row in `docs/WORK_PACKAGES.md:36`, the S20-310 closeout, the machine-summary section's `implementation` list (`root_query.rs` only), and `scripts/check_root_backed_query_profile.py` (which knows only `root_query.rs`, `run_root_query`, and `conformance/root-backed-query`). Meanwhile `machine-summary.json:120` says AT-MW-02 is "owned by S20-410" and still claims the methods do not exist. Ownership is declared in one direction only, and the section checker cannot see what the section now claims to own.

That matters for this verdict. The field being adjudicated carries `FAIL_2_P0_7_P1_5_P2_4_P3` from the 2026-09-04 round on `root_query.rs` and the contract. The two P0s are recorded closed in `p0_closed` (revision 3). The seven P1s have no closure ledger, and I verified on `0bcc9c6` that at least four remain open: P1-1 (arm-1 snapshot is `RootMismatch` in `verify()` at `root_query.rs:224-228` but `ProfileUnsupported` at `:671-672`; spec section 1 rule 1 still contradicts section 8 step 2 and section 9), P1-2 (section 3 still asserts page union without a valid page-sequence predicate), P1-5 (no generating principle for the class set), and P1-7 (the checker extracts the revision at `:176` but never asserts it against `contract_revision`). P1-4 is closed by the revision 2 table. Issuing a same-lane superseding PASS on this field from an entity-read-only scope would launder those open P1s into a PASS record. I decline to do that; the entity-read surface passes its own axes, and the section field reads REVISE until the register carries either a P1 ledger for the prior round or a separate section for the entity-read surface.
