Two architecture defects keep this from PASS. First, the section owns the *derivation* of the record, and the record committed at the re-mint cannot be reached through the owned builder plus the documented runbook: the carry-forward rule and the staleness rule contradict each other at any re-mint over a multi-host report, and the operator had to break that deadlock by hand without a receipt. Neither is a P1 because the authoritative fields are correct and checker-bound; both are P2 because the section's whole promise is that reproducibility facts are derived, not asserted. The lab lease (external, until 2026-09-21T03:24Z) is not a defect of this section. No GA or release-readiness claim is made or implied.
