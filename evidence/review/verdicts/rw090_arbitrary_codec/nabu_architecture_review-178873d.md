## Assessment

The structural claims of RW-090 hold: the decode leg is genuinely arbitrary for all 18 kinds (17 schema recipes plus the fully decoded fixed DependencyBinding), no digest-pinned or fixed-witness path is reachable from the arbitrary images, the bounded generation is cleanly segregated behind separate constructors and `bounded_*` tests, and every current and superseded figure I cross-checked is consistent across the four manifests, the S verifier, the records and the pinned test assertions; all four checkers pass at HEAD, and official C1 is correctly kept null for reasons the records state accurately. Ownership and dependency direction are sound (codec stays a leaf; the composer/schema legs are unchanged; identity namespaces are proven disjoint).

What blocks acceptance is oracle discipline, not structure. The arbitrary decoders diverge from the native reference at the nesting bound (TypeExpr embedding depth not threaded; const-value depth unpinned and map-order precedence deviating), the divergence is fail-open (Sley accepts, native refuses) and the encode leg would emit such objects; the per-kind refusal tests pin author-chosen codes rather than the oracle's verdicts; and the same construction nonce/ordinals were reused across generations without reconciling contract §3. The records are honest about the encode leg and the C1 status but under-carry the known parity deviations into the aggregate records and the component manifest, and one governing record (rw-080-canonical-s.md) now contradicts itself. The encode leg being "arbitrary" only at the body-bytes level is disclosed and does not change the verdict on its own.
